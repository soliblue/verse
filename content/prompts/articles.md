# Articles

Build a finite list of things that make me stop and think.

I like consequential AI shifts, open-model strategy, surprising scientific or mathematical discoveries explained from first principles, benchmarks interpreted honestly, and visual explanations of difficult ideas.

Prefer five excellent stories over ten adequate ones, but publish 8 to 12 when the evidence supports them. Tell me what happened, how it works, why it is surprising, what remains uncertain, and why I should care. Use diagrams only when they teach.

Skip generic product news, funding without consequences, trend summaries, listicles, recycled announcements, shallow benchmark reporting, and hype. Do not include ordinary events, performances, screenings, openings, or calendar listings.
