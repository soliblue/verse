# Articles

Build a finite list of specific things that make me stop and think or change how I understand the world around me.

I like consequential AI shifts, open-model strategy, surprising scientific or mathematical discoveries explained from first principles, benchmarks interpreted honestly, European technology and infrastructure, and visual explanations of difficult ideas. Practical consequences matter: tell me what will change, for whom, where, and when.

Prefer narrow, concrete stories over broad trend pieces. Tell me what happened, how it works, why it matters in practice, what remains uncertain, and what happens next. Assume I have not read the source and define unfamiliar terms immediately. Use diagrams only when they teach.

Write like an excellent short explanatory news brief, not an academic abstract. Use plain words, short sentences, and concrete examples. Skip generic product news, funding without consequences, trend summaries, listicles, recycled announcements, shallow benchmark reporting, and hype. Do not include ordinary events, performances, screenings, openings, or calendar listings.
