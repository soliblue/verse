# Benchmark the craft behind a monster voice

> A new dataset pairs human and animal vocal sources with professionally processed monster, robot, and other non-human voice designs. Its controlled splits test whether conversion systems learn a reusable transformation or merely recognize familiar voices and presets.

Most public voice-conversion benchmarks focus on natural human speech. That leaves film, audiobook, and game vocal effects without a shared way to compare systems.

The dataset applies effect presets to varied speech, animal sounds, interjections, and mimicry sources. Its test design separates seen from unseen source-timbre groups and seen from unseen preset styles, making generalization failures easier to locate.

The paper provides baseline results and demo samples and has been accepted at Interspeech 2026. The benchmarks are still produced by the dataset authors, and reuse must respect the source-level licenses attached to individual recordings.

## Why this was selected

It turns a skilled sound-design problem into a reproducible research object without pretending that one aggregate score captures the whole craft.
