# MeluXina-AI contract adds 1,008 GPUs to Europe’s shared compute

> EuroHPC has signed an €80 million contract for an AI supercomputer in Luxembourg with 1,008 Nvidia Blackwell GPUs. Startups, smaller companies, and researchers are intended users, so the practical test is whether shared European compute becomes easier to access.

E4 Computer Engineering will acquire, install, and maintain MeluXina-AI. EuroHPC will fund half of the €80 million budget, with installation expected to start in autumn 2026.

The system will spread 252 liquid-cooled GB200 NVL4 nodes across two Luxembourg sites. EuroHPC estimates a peak AI performance above 20 exaflops and says the machine will support training, testing, and pre-production deployment through isolated multi-tenant environments.

The headline performance is an estimate, not a measured application result. EuroHPC has not given an operational date, so prospective users still do not know when the promised capacity will become available.

## Why this was selected

It turns European “sovereign AI” from a strategy document into a contracted machine with a price, hardware count, access model, and installation window.
